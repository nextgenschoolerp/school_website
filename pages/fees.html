Implementation & Compliance

Frequently asked questions about Fee Management

Clear answers on compliance, multi-campus governance, and institutional policies.

01

Can we support different fee structures across different campuses, boards, or shifts?

Yes. Knowva natively isolates fee heads by campus, affiliation board (e.g. CBSE vs ICSE), stream, and shift while enabling single-window consolidated trust-level financial reporting and head-office audit trails.

02

How does Knowva handle sibling discounts and role-governed fee concessions?

Sibling relationships are mapped automatically from Student Master records via parent contact coordinates. Concession rules (such as 15% discount on tuition for the second child, or staff ward waivers) are applied automatically to generated demand schedules with an immutable audit log.

03

Can a student receive their Transfer Certificate (TC) if fee dues remain pending?

No. Knowva enforces an institutional hard gate. The Documents & Certificates module flags pending dues and completely locks TC issuance until the Finance Cashier signs off a digital "No Dues Clearance Certificate".
