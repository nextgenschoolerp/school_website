Enterprise security specs

Built for students' data — and your IT team's sign-off

A school holds minors' records; they deserve real controls. Role-based access on every action, a full audit trail of who did what, multi-tenant isolation between campuses, encrypted integration secrets and OIDC-based delivery — documented control by control, not hand-waved.

Data protection & isolation

Encrypted integration secrets

Integration secrets (SMTP, SMS, WhatsApp) are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Tenant and campus scoping is enforced by signed claims on every request.

Granular field-level permissions

Role-based access control (RBAC)

A permission matrix means teachers, accountants and staff only see the data their job requires — and the server returns 403 for anything else.

Activity tracking

Audit logs

Every change records user, IP, action, resource and outcome. Role changes have their own log, and records carry created-by and updated-by fields.

Privacy by design

Privacy standards

Built around the principles of FERPA, GDPR and India’s DPDP Act. We do not claim certifications; we share our control evidence on request.

Roadmap

Backups & recovery

Backup and restore are supported as configurable policy today. Full automation and tested recovery targets are on our roadmap.
