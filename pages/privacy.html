Data protection & privacy policy

Privacy built for students, staff, and school governance

A school ERP processes minors' personal records, academic histories, biometric attendance, and staff payroll. We treat privacy as an architecture requirement, not legal disclaimers. Here is how Knowva Systems handles, isolates, and protects institutional data.

Governance model

Who owns the data: Fiduciary vs. Processor

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023) and global educational privacy frameworks (FERPA and GDPR), legal roles must be unambiguous.

The School is the Data Fiduciary

Your educational institution (school, trust, or society) determines why student, parent, and staff data is collected, how long it is retained, and which roles have authorization to view or edit it. The school maintains complete legal ownership and control of its records.

Knowva Systems is the Data Processor

Knowva Systems provides the multi-tenant SaaS application and processing infrastructure strictly as an agent of the school under a binding contract. We process personal data solely to deliver the ERP platform and never use it for independent commercial purposes or third-party profiling.

Zero commercial monetization pledge: Knowva Systems does not sell, license, broker, or monetize student, parent, or staff data under any circumstances. We do not display external advertisements, run behavioral tracking, or build marketing profiles.

Data categories

What information the ERP processes

The platform collects only what is strictly necessary to run school operations, board reporting (CBSE, ICSE, State Boards), parent communication, and statutory staff payroll.

Student personal & academic records

Information managed across Admissions, Students, and Academics:

  • Full name, date of birth, gender, blood group, emergency contacts
  • Enrollment number, roll number, class, section, academic house
  • Daily attendance, exam scores, CBSE grade sheets, progress cards
  • Transfer & bonafide certificates, conduct notes, health/allergy alerts

Parent & guardian information

Information required for family communications and fee ledger management:

  • Parent/guardian names, relationship, residential address
  • Phone numbers for WhatsApp/SMS emergency circulars and notices
  • Email addresses for electronic receipts and student newsletters
  • Fee demand statements, receipts, scholarship records, concession logs

Staff & employee data

Information required for HR operations, teaching allocation, and statutory payroll:

  • Employee ID, department, designation, qualifications, joining date
  • Biometric attendance punch summaries and leave approvals
  • Salary structures, bank disbursement details, PF/ESI/TDS tax deductions
  • Encrypted PDF payslip delivery history

Technical safeguards

Privacy by design architecture

Security and privacy are engineered directly into the software stack to ensure confidentiality, integrity, and non-repudiation.

Privacy & Data Security Specifications
Data residency Stored in certified Amazon Web Services (AWS) data centres located in India (Mumbai region).
Multi-tenant isolation Each school and campus is cryptographically partitioned via signed tenant and branch claims. Data cannot leak between institutions or unauthorized branches.
Access control Server-authoritative Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Permissions are verified on every API request. Unauthorized actions return HTTP 403.
Encryption in transit All web and API traffic is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
Encryption at rest Sensitive credentials (SMTP, SMS gateways, WhatsApp API keys) and databases use industry-standard AES-256-GCM encryption.
Central audit trail Immutable logging of user identity, IP address, timestamp, action taken, and target record. Dual sign-offs are enforced for critical changes like certificates and exam unlock tickets.

Data lifecycle & user rights

Retention, principal rights, and sub-processors

Clear guidelines on how long institutional records are retained, how parents and staff can exercise their legal rights, and which infrastructure partners we use.

Data retention & archival

Institutional records are retained for the duration of the school’s active contract and in alignment with educational board compliance rules (e.g., maintaining academic transcripts and attendance registers). Upon contract conclusion, the school has a 30-day export window, after which tenant databases are cryptographically purged.

Rights of Data Principals

Under the DPDP Act 2023, parents, students (via guardians), and staff have rights to access, review, and request correction of inaccurate records. Since the school is the Data Fiduciary, data principals should direct requests to the school administration. We provide schools with the tools to fulfill these requests immediately.

Sub-processors & integrations

We work with enterprise infrastructure sub-processors that maintain strict data security standards: AWS India for hosting and Kubernetes compute. External communication channels (SMS gateways, WhatsApp, SMTP) run via school-configured credentials or vetted telecommunication partners.
