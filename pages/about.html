01 • Architecture

Role-first

Each person sees their job, everyone else is kept out.

Every interface in Knowva is custom-molded to one of 8 distinct administrative desks: Teacher, Registrar, Accountant, Transport Manager, Librarian, Examination Controller, Principal, and Trustee. Data entered once and trusted everywhere

When an applicant is confirmed during admissions, their record immediately populates the class register, fee ledger, transport route, health record, and national UDISE+ compliance portal. No manual export-import routines, zero duplicate profiles, and no sync lag between systems.

Oversight and isolation for multi-campus groups

School management trusts need rolled-up financial reporting, consolidated staff payroll, and centralized fee analytics. At the same time, individual branches require complete privacy, isolated student rosters, and separate banking credentials. Knowva provides both without compromise.
