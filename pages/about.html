About us

We build the system schools actually run on

Knowva Systems makes School ERP — one platform that carries a school from an admission enquiry to a graduate's transfer certificate, built for the registrars, teachers, accountants and principals who use it every day.

Our mission

To replace the spreadsheets, paper registers and disconnected tools that run most schools with a single, role-aware system any staff member can learn in a morning.

Why school ERP

Schools juggle admissions, records, academics, fees, HR and communication in silos. Knowva connects them on one core, so data is entered once and trusted everywhere. Multi-campus groups and trusts need oversight and isolation — our tenancy model gives both. And compliance, from CBSE grading to statutory payroll returns to auditable marks, is built in rather than bolted on.

What we believe

Role-first

The software should show each person their job and keep everyone else out — by default, on every action.

Accountable

Who did what, and why, should always be answerable: audit trails, change logs, dual sign-offs.

Honest

We document what the product does, screen by screen. Our user manual is written from the live app.
