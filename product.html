The product

One platform, thirteen connected modules

Built on a shared, role-aware core — so a student enrolled in Admissions appears in Students, bills through Fees, and shows up in Attendance and Academics without re-keying anything.

Workspace

01

Overview & dashboards

A role-shaped command centre — every login lands on the data and tasks that are theirs.

  • Role dashboards: School, HR, Academics, Teaching
  • A worklist that deep-links straight to the action
02

Students Live

A 360° record for every child, from enrolment to transfer.

  • Enrol, promote a class, conduct & medical, ID cards
  • Bonafide & transfer certificates with dual sign-off
03

Admissions Live

One pipeline from first enquiry to first day of school.

  • Counselling, entrance exams, offers, seat deposits
  • Admissions Outlook: seat demand & what converts
04

Staff & faculty Live

Hire, assign and support every staff member.

  • Onboarding, leave approvals, compliance documents
  • Self-service "About me": duties, leave, payslips
05

Payroll Live

Run payroll, file returns, pay staff — compliantly.

  • Gross-to-net with PF, ESI and TDS
  • Statutory returns & encrypted payslips
06

Academics & exams Live

Marks in, results out — with a lock and an audit trail.

  • CBSE marks grid, timed lock/unlock tickets
  • Broadsheets, progress cards, immutable change log
07

Teaching & planning Live

The timetable builds itself; the day runs itself.

  • Conflict-free timetable generator & auto cover
  • Syllabus, homework, lesson plans, lab booking
08

Attendance Live

Registers that close on time, and tell you who's slipping.

  • Daily / AM-PM registers with a 7-day lock window
  • Biometric staff punches & 95%-target analytics
09

Fees & payments Live

Bill a class, chase a defaulter, close the books.

  • Fee heads & schedules, post demands to a class
  • Receipts, late fees, defaulter tracking

School management

10

Communications

Reach every parent on the channel they read.

  • Circulars WhatsApp→SMS/Email, emergency alerts
  • Gate passes, visitors, grievances
11

Calendar

Term dates, events and parent meetings, shared.

  • Month/week calendar with real term dates
  • PT appointment slots, iCal export
12

Reports

Build the report once, run it whenever.

  • Custom report builder across every module
  • Trustee KPIs & CBSE board-compliance exports
13

Documents & admin

The document vault and the control room.

  • Permissioned document library with access history
  • Roles & access, integrations, bulk data import
How the modules fit together: Admissions creates a Student → billed in Fees, tracked in Attendance and Academics, reached through Communications and Calendar, reported in Reports — all under one roles & access model set in Settings.
